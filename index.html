Sounding Home

an interactive digital sound archive

This archive is an artistic synthesis combining fieldwork and research about environmental sounds. The aim is to create a space where acoustic data in the form of an archive creatively engage you. The page is built as the result of a protocol I have made for archiving and I want you to be able to explore the concept of 'being home' or 'building a habitat' through sound via an active participation, hence 'sound' becoming the verb 'sounding'.

You can drag any recording from my archive into the house and create your own soundscape composition. A small echoing Pocket of the world. When the audio files are dragged into the house you can choose between two parameters: loudness and reverberation. A sound placed at the bottom is loud, on top it is quiet. To the left it will be with reverb (distant) and to the right the sound remains dry (nearby). The sounding environment will become three-dimensional when multiple sounds are added, and the sounds begin to relate to each other.

The experience is best with headphones.

On the bottom you may describe your soundscape composition and download it. The download will be a ZIP-file containing a 20 second audio file and a json with your description.

Sounding home is a symbiotic fusion between the act of archiving, habitat listening, the imagining of acoustic spaces and language exploration for environmental sounds.
